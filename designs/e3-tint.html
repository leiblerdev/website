A model that only has to be good at your tickets beats one that has to be good at everything.

  • CostA small model specialised on one job runs on a fraction of the compute. Same work, a tenth of the bill.
  • LatencyFewer parameters, shorter prompts, no queue behind the whole world. A reply in 200 milliseconds instead of four seconds.
  • AccuracyIt has seen a thousand of your tickets and your rules, not the whole internet. It stops guessing what you meant.

Questions

Short answers to the three we hear most.

Does it work for my workflow?

If your agent does the same kind of task thousands of times a week and you can say what a good answer looks like, yes. Bring one task and we evaluate it for free.

What happens when a better frontier model comes out?

The agent re-runs your eval against it the day it ships. If the new model wins on your tasks, you get a report. If yours still wins, nothing changes.

How long until it is live?

Evaluation in 48 hours. A trained, hosted model in about a week.
