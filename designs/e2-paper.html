Latency

Questions

Does it work for my workflow?

If your agent does the same kind of task thousands of times a week and you can say what a good answer looks like, yes. Bring one task and we evaluate it for free.

What happens when a better frontier model comes out?

The agent re-runs your eval against it the day it ships. If the new model wins on your tasks, you get a report. If yours still wins, nothing changes.

How long until it is live?

Evaluation in 48 hours. A trained, hosted model in about a week.
