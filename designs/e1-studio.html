Why a model of your own.

Frontier models carry everything.

They answer your refund email with the weight of the whole internet, and you pay for all of it every time.

Your agent does one thing.

The same task, a million times a month. It needs depth on that task, not breadth.

A specialised model wins on it.

Trained on your traces and checked against your bar, it is cheaper, faster and more accurate on your tickets than the frontier.

Meet Kullback, the agent that builds it.

Say you run a support agent on refund emails. You tell Kullback how the model should behave. It writes the eval and the training data, trains, hosts, and keeps watching the frontier. You never touch a GPU.
