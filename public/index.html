Meet Kullback

The agent that builds it

Our open source agent. You describe a behavior; it builds the model.

  • Reads your traces, knowledge graph and, with permission, your agent's code.

  • Turns the behavior you type into eval tasks, then grades every frontier model.

  • Generates training data for the same scenarios. You review samples first.

  • Post-trains a 9B to 27B model with RL until it clears your bar.

  • Hosts it, and re-runs the eval when a new frontier model ships.

How it works

Synthetic data that matches production

Kullback rebuilds your agent's world from these inputs. Then three agents harden every task.

  • Your traces
  • Business knowledge graph
  • Your request
  • GitHub and agent harness with permission
  1. 01

    Builder

    Rebuilds your agent's environment: data, tools, rules, users.

  2. 02

    Examiner

    Writes a check for each task and sends fixes back to the Builder.

  3. 03

    Writer

    Turns passing runs into eval and training data you can read.

Questions

What people ask

What is leibler?

A data lab. We post-train small models on your data that beat the frontier on your tasks.

Why not just use a frontier model?

It's built for everyone. Yours is 10 to 100 times smaller, so it's cheaper, faster and more accurate on your work.

What is Kullback?

Our open source agent. It writes the eval, generates the training data and trains the model. Apache-2.0.

Why trust synthetic data?

It's built from your traces, knowledge graph and agent code, and every task is checked before it's used.

Does my data leave my account?

No. The model is yours to keep.

How long does it take?

Eval in 48 hours. A hosted model in about a week.

Who is behind leibler?

Krrish Agarwalla, a post-training researcher at Stanford and LMU Munich.
