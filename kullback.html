kullback is the open-source half of the harness.

Your traces in, a verified environment out.

Kullback rebuilds the world your agent works in from the traces it already produced, checks the rebuild by replaying those traces, and grades any model in it on what it changed. Apache-2.0, on GitHub.

01

What it builds.

From a week of traces: the rows your runs touched, one function per tool that behaves the way the real tool was observed to behave, your policy rules compiled into checks, a simulated user who knows what the real user knew, and a verifier per task.

Every value points back to the byte in your trace it came from. Nothing in the environment exists without a trace behind it.

02

How it is checked.

Before any model is graded, the rebuild has to reproduce the recorded runs, on runs it never saw. First measurement, on Sierra's public tau2 retail traces, where the real tools and database exist to compare against.

Verdicts are code over end state. A judge model can narrow the bar, never award a pass. Runs that needed a model stand-in for any tool are reported, never counted.

03

What is public.

The code, the design, the decision log with every choice and the alternative it beat, 29 research reports, and the list of what is not built yet. Contributions go through pull requests; the maintainer reviews every one.
